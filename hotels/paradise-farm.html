Paradise Farm Cottages

Nestled in the scenic highlands of Nyeri, Paradise Farm Cottages offers a tranquil escape surrounded by lush greenery, the gentle sound of waterfalls, and crisp mountain air.

Our charming retreat combines rustic elegance with modern comfort. From farm-to-table dining to guided nature trails and authentic farm experiences — this is where families reconnect, couples unwind, and nature lovers find their paradise.
