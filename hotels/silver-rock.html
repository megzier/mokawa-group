Welcome To

Silver Rock Boutique Resort

Nestled on the pristine shores of Malindi, Silver Rock Boutique Resort stands as a beacon of coastal luxury and exceptional hospitality. Winner of the Best-in-Class Heritage Hospitality Awards 2024, we offer an unparalleled beachfront experience.

Experience the perfect blend of modern luxury and traditional Swahili hospitality. From our boutique in-house spa to our ocean-view restaurant serving fresh coastal cuisine, every detail is crafted to create unforgettable memories.
