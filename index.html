Transforming Hospitality in East Africa

Mokawa Group is a fast-growing hospitality management and leasing company headquartered in Nairobi, Kenya, specializing in leasing, operating, and transforming underutilized hospitality assets into thriving destinations.

Our Core Values: We manage every property with an ownership mindset, ensuring operational excellence and profitability. Transparency and trust are at the heart of everything we do—clear financials and real partnerships define our relationships. With agility at our core, we execute quickly and adapt smartly to market changes, turning challenges into opportunities for growth.

Our Mission

To lease, manage, and scale hospitality assets into high-yielding, guest-centric businesses that owners can take pride in.

Our Vision

To become Africa's most respected boutique hotel leasing and management company—delivering smart hospitality solutions and turning overlooked assets into profitable, admired destinations.
